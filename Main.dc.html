About Me

Hello, fellow vegans and/or lifters! I'm Suzy, aka The Vegan Mimi, and I've been lifting on and off (sadly, mostly “off”) since high school. I've picked it back up again over the past few years, to strengthen my legs for knee replacement surgery. Most recently, as of the date of creation of this site (August 2026), I've returned with a renewed purpose. Strong legs isn't enough; I'm working on overall body strength.

You see, I don't care about bulk, or toning, or “getting cut” — I'm focusing on sheer, brute strength. I don't let the fact that I'm ahem getting on in years deter me from showing up at the gym and giving it my all. But, because I'm still healing from two surgeries and getting used to two new knees, I have to be more aware of my limitations (neither of my knees will ever again have full flexion).

I became a vegetarian in 1980, and fully vegan in 2011. Although the underlying reason has always been animal welfare, I have to admit that the health benefits have been the lagniappe. Hypercholesterolemia runs in my family, and I've been able to avoid it through my diet. The routine labs drawn before each of my knee replacements showed excellent metabolic health, and my EKGs were normal.
